Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project.

How are files sent and delivered? +

All raw footage reception and final 4K master video delivery are handled smoothly through dedicated Google Drive folders. You simply share your Drive link, and I deliver your finished exports directly into your folder.

How long does it take to deliver the first cut? +

For short-form reels and individual videos, the first draft is delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Larger commercial or AI-heavy projects typically take 3 to 5 business days depending on footage scope.

Are revisions included in the price? +

Yes! Every project includes up to 2 rounds of revisions at no additional cost to guarantee that the final export matches your creative vision 100%.

Which editing and AI software do you use? +

I work with industry-standard professional software: Adobe Premiere Pro for editing & pacing, DaVinci Resolve Studio for Hollywood-grade color, After Effects for motion graphics, and Runway Gen-3 & Midjourney for generative AI visuals.
